### Russell White

I build tools for learning, forecasting and deciding under risk. Economics student at CU Boulder from January 2027.

**In daily use**

- [study-reader](https://github.com/JapanColorado/study-reader): a Linux reader for PDFs, EPUBs and web pages that puts the whole book in the conversation with Claude and turns quizzes into spaced-repetition questions.
- [fatebook-android](https://github.com/JapanColorado/fatebook-android): an unofficial Android client for the [Fatebook](https://fatebook.io) prediction tracker, with offline-first sync and calibration analytics.
- [Yagi](https://github.com/JapanColorado/Yagi): a podcast and audiobook player with no ads or trackers, on-device ad skipping and a voice-clarity filter.
- [mood-sampler-android](https://github.com/JapanColorado/mood-sampler-android): a mood and time-use tracker that asks at random moments, so the data stays honest.
